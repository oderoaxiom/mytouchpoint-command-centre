Executive Summary

August performance was mixed across the region. Kenya and Uganda registered strong growth across Transactions, Volumes and Revenue (standardised to XOF), while Nigeria and Tanzania experienced declines driven by operational disruptions and market challenges.

Key External Events Impacting Performance
  • Ebola outbreak in Uganda (June-August 2026)
  • Wave deactivation across the network (July-August 2026)
  • Deactivation of the Nigerian corridor during the last week of August 2026

Country Highlights

🇰🇪 Kenya
Continues to lead regional transaction growth, accounting for 50% of all transactions YTD. August saw a strong rebound across all KPIs (with volumes and revenue standardised in XOF), though revenue yield remains relatively low compared to other markets.
🇺🇬 Uganda
Despite Ebola-related disruptions, Uganda delivered the strongest month-on-month growth in volumes and revenue (measured in XOF), demonstrating strong market resilience and recovery potential.
🇳🇬 Nigeria
Remains the region's largest volume market (in XOF terms). August performance was impacted by the Nigerian corridor deactivation and the Wave service disruption, resulting in declines across all KPIs.
🇹🇿 Tanzania
Maintains leadership in revenue generation, contributing 41% of total regional revenue YTD (standardised to XOF). However, August performance softened significantly and requires close monitoring.

Management Priorities for Q4 2026

  • ✅ Sustain Kenya's transaction growth while improving revenue yield.
  • ✅ Accelerate Uganda's current growth momentum.
  • ⚠ Stabilize Nigeria following corridor and network disruptions.
  • ⚠ Restore transaction growth in Tanzania while preserving revenue leadership.
Executive Conclusion

Kenya and Uganda demonstrated strong resilience and growth in August, offsetting some of the operational challenges experienced in Nigeria and Tanzania. While Kenya remains the region's transaction leader, Nigeria continues to lead volumes and Tanzania remains the largest revenue contributor (all financial figures standardised to XOF). Focus during Q4 should be centered on sustaining momentum in growth markets while accelerating recovery initiatives in impacted corridors and channels.
