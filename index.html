Executive Conclusion

Kenya and Uganda demonstrated strong resilience and growth in August, offsetting some of the operational challenges experienced in Nigeria and Tanzania. While Kenya remains the region's transaction leader, Nigeria continues to lead volumes and Tanzania remains the largest revenue contributor (all financial figures standardised to XOF). Focus during Q4 should be centered on sustaining momentum in growth markets while accelerating recovery initiatives in impacted corridors and channels.
