Stage 2: Payout Execution & Variance -40.33 KES Drag

FX Loss Calculation: At the true market baseline rate of 27.65 UGX/KES, procuring that exact 28,765 UGX payout requires 1,040.33 KES in Cost of Sales (28,765 / 27.65). Subtracting the available 1,000.00 KES net conversion pool from the 1,040.33 KES Cost of Sales results in an FX variance loss of -40.33 KES.
↓
Stage 3: Razor-Thin Margin Buffer +12.30 KES Net
Gross fee revenue (52.63 KES) minus the FX settlement slippage (40.33 KES) leaves an extremely vulnerable initial net profit margin buffer of only +12.30 KES.
↓
Stage 4: Treasury Repatriation Wipeout NET DEFICIT (-0.30 KES)
Accumulated revenue pooled in Dakar (Senegal) must be wired back to Kenya in KES.
Mathematical Breakdown of the Wipeout:
• What the 1.2% represents: The 1.2% wire fee is charged on the base settlement pool (1,000.00 KES pool or transferred base).
• The Exact Calculation: 1.2% of the 1,000.00 KES base pool equals exactly 12.00 KES.
• The Final Wipeout: Subtracting this 12.00 KES repatriation wire fee from the slim +12.30 KES profit buffer leaves a remaining net profit of only +0.30 KES per transaction. When factoring in standard bank wire handling charges or flat intermediate banking switching fees, the margin is entirely wiped out into a net operational loss or near-zero deficit, rendering high-frequency retail corridor expansion unsustainable.
